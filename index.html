Why this and not the alternatives

The finance is the product. The technology is how it gets delivered.

vs. your bookkeeper

Bookkeeping tells you what happened. I work on what it means and what to do — pricing, margin, runway, risk. Both are needed; they are not the same job.

vs. a traditional accountant

Historical reporting is the starting point, not the deliverable. Forecasting, scenarios and decision support are where the value sits.

vs. an AI freelancer

You already have access to AI. What you are buying is the financial logic it has to be pointed at — cost structure, allocation, controls, judgement. AI is my leverage, not my offer.

vs. a dashboard developer

A dashboard is a delivery layer. The real question is which decision it enables, and whether the numbers behind it are constructed correctly.

Before you send a stranger your numbers

Working remotely with financial data, safely.

No banking credentials. Ever.

I never ask for bank logins or payment authority. Read-only exports from whatever system you already use are enough.

Anonymise before you send

Customer and employee names can be replaced with codes. The analysis works identically on anonymised data — I need the structure, not the identities.

NDA first, if you prefer

Happy to sign yours before the first call. If you do not have one, I will send a short mutual NDA.

“We don’t have those reports”

Almost nobody does. If the raw data exists — invoices, ledger exports, payroll, a spreadsheet — the reports can be reconstructed from it.

“We don’t use QuickBooks”

Fine. Xero, Zoho, Sage, an ERP, or a folder of spreadsheets. I work from exports, so the software you use is not a constraint.

Clear professional boundaries

Advisory and internal-audit work — not statutory audit, external assurance, legal or tax advice. Stated plainly so nobody is misled. Scope
