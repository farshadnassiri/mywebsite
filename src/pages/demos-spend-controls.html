What management does with this

Recover

Duplicate payments are a direct cash recovery — usually the fastest return an owner gets from any finance project.

Reset the limit

If invoices cluster just under the approval limit, the limit is not working. Change the threshold, or approve by vendor rather than by invoice.

Split the duties

In a small team one person often enters and approves. That is normal — but it needs a compensating control, such as an owner review of the weekly payment run.

Make it repeatable

The value is not one report. It is these tests running every month on the current export, so exceptions surface while they are still fixable.
