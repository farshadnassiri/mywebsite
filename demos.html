Interactive demos

Claims are cheap. These let you check the reasoning.

Each of these is a working model, not a screenshot. Change the inputs and the conclusions change with them — including the conclusions that are inconvenient. That is the honest test of whether the financial logic underneath is real.

Pricing · Margin01

Pricing & margin simulator

Change price and customer sensitivity; see revenue, gross margin, operating profit and break-even move together. Includes the maximum volume you can afford to lose before a price rise stops paying, and a ranking of which 1% — price, volume, variable cost, fixed cost — is worth the most.

Open the simulator
Cash · Forecasting02

13-week cash flow & runway

Weekly projection driven by growth, collection days, gross margin, payroll timing and a one-off payment. Flags the trough week, the funding gap against your safety floor, and what a hiring decision does to the floor rather than to the P&L.

Open the cash model
Profit · Costing03

Client profitability & margin leakage

Ten clients, one overhead pool, three allocation bases. Switch between them and watch the ranking rearrange. Includes the question every owner eventually asks — “should we fire this client?” — with the answer overhead allocation usually hides.

Open the margin model
Controls · Internal audit04

Spend controls & audit analytics

A month of payments run through five control tests: duplicates, approval-limit splitting, segregation of duties, off-hours postings and new-vendor risk. Full population, adjustable thresholds, value at risk quantified.

Run the tests

Why the data is synthetic — and why that is stated on every page

Client work is confidential, and inventing case studies to look established is not a foundation for a finance relationship. These models are built on constructed data that behaves like a real small business. The method, the arithmetic and the interpretation are exactly what an engagement produces; only the company is imaginary. As clients agree to be referenced, anonymised real outcomes will sit alongside them.
