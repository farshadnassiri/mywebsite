What this looks like as an engagement

The demo assumes one product and one cost per unit. Real pricing work starts by establishing what a unit actually costs you — including the overhead nobody allocates — then builds this model on your product mix, your customer segments and your contract terms. The output is a price decision you can defend to a customer, not a percentage.
