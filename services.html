Remote Internal Audit

Scoped per engagement

An independent review of how a specific process actually operates — procurement, payroll, revenue, expenses or inventory — tested against the full transaction population rather than a sample.

  • Process walkthrough and control mapping
  • Data-driven testing across 100% of transactions
  • Duplicate payments and approval-threshold anomalies
  • Unusual journals, off-hours postings, round-sum entries
  • Findings rated by risk and quantified in money
  • Root cause, not just the exception
  • Practical remediation sized for your team

Scope boundary, stated plainly. This is internal audit — a management service. It is not a statutory or external audit, not an assurance engagement, and produces no opinion on financial statements. Where a statutory audit is required, you need a licensed audit firm, and I will say so.

Questions people ask before the first call

Why is a project $2,000 when a bookkeeper costs less per month?

Because the deliverables are different things. Bookkeeping records what happened; this work changes what happens next. A pricing review that lifts realised margin by two points on $2M of revenue is worth $40,000 a year, permanently. The fee should be judged against the decision it improves, not against an hourly rate.

If a project cannot be connected to money, risk or a decision, I will tell you that before you buy it.

We already have an accountant. Does this replace them?

No — and it should not. Your accountant handles compliance, statutory filings and tax. I work on the management side: what the numbers mean and what to do about them. The two roles complement each other, and I am happy to work alongside whoever you already use.

How much of your work is AI?

AI is how some of the work gets done faster; it is not what you are buying. You already have access to the same tools. What you are paying for is knowing which financial question to ask, how a cost should be allocated, which control actually matters, and whether an answer is wrong — judgement that does not come out of a prompt.

Anything sensitive is handled deliberately, and I will tell you exactly which tools touch your data if you ask.

We do not use QuickBooks. Is that a problem?

No. Xero, Zoho, Sage, MYOB, an ERP, or a folder of spreadsheets — I work from exports, so the underlying software is not a constraint. If your system will produce a CSV of transactions, that is enough to begin.

Our records are incomplete. Should we wait until they are tidy?

No, and waiting is how a year goes by. If the raw data exists — invoices, ledger exports, payroll, bank statements — cash flow statements, AR ageing and costing analyses can be reconstructed from it. Reconstructing them is frequently the first deliverable, and it is often the moment an owner sees their business clearly for the first time.

How does an engagement actually start?

A 30-minute call about the decision you are facing. If I can help, you get a fixed-fee proposal in writing with a defined scope and delivery date. If I cannot, I will say so and point you somewhere better. Nothing starts before you have agreed the scope and price.

Not sure which one you need?

That is the normal starting position, and it is what the Financial Health Review exists to answer. Or describe the problem in a sentence and I will tell you which service fits — including if the answer is “none of them, yet”.
